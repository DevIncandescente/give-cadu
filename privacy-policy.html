Privacy Policy

How Cado handles information shared while creating and joining celebrations.

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Overview

Cado helps hosts create celebration pages and helps guests contribute to meaningful gifts. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

Information We May Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, event details, guest messages, contribution details, and account information. We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, device information, and usage activity to keep the site working properly.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage celebrations, process guest interactions, provide support, improve the experience, prevent misuse, and communicate important updates related to the service.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the platform, process payments, deliver messages, or keep the service secure. Hosts may see information that guests choose to share with a celebration.

Payments

Payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers. Cado should not store full card details directly unless explicitly stated by the payment provider flow.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information where applicable. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Cado team through the support channel provided on the website.
